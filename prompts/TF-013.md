spec register: the pacino LLM specs over the TF-010 equal-weight pool

0.74) from high to low. `ALTERNATIVE_FRAMING` and `af_rather_than` keep
their levels.

phrasing, and `PARTICIPIAL_TAIL` rose 3.5 times in arXiv bodies while
