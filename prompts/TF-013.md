## Hypothesis
H1. The `TRICOLON` LR holds for each label. Each of `enumeration`,
`list-like` and `figure` with at least 20 findings on both sides has an
LR interval whose lower end is above 1, so set C gives each label at
least med.

H2. A level change changes no finding. Before and after, every corpus
run gives the same findings with the same spans and fingerprints;
only the confidence field differs. Harvest labels and totals are
identical, and no waiver goes stale.

H3. Raising `TRICOLON` costs precision at med in the blog register.
Harvest's prec* over findings reported at med or above falls, because
`TRICOLON` findings are mostly accepted (84 accepted, 19 rewritten,
prec* 0.18). Report the size of the drop, whatever it is.

Report each outcome as found, including null and failed hypotheses.

## Background
Confidence levels were set by judgement and then by the TF-007 interim
rule over the arXiv ratios. TF-012 measured LRs for the design and
spec register: the pacino LLM specs over the TF-010 equal-weight pool
of pre-2022 human specs. It proposed three cut sets over the raw LR;
set C gives med when the interval's lower end is above 1 and high when
it is 3 or more.

Under set C, of the six unflagged rows, `TRICOLON` and `tricolon`
(LR 2.02, 1.83 to 2.25) go from low to med, `EMPHATIC_REFLEXIVE` (0.87,
0.60 to 1.18) from med to low, and `PARTICIPIAL_TAIL` (0.54, 0.36 to
0.74) from high to low. `ALTERNATIVE_FRAMING` and `af_rather_than` keep
their levels.

The LLM corpus is steered: pacino's instructions discourage decorative
phrasing, and `PARTICIPIAL_TAIL` rose 3.5 times in arXiv bodies while
running low in the steered specs. The user kept it at high.

Section 5.3 asks for two measurements before a confident finding: the
LR, and whether the construction intrudes, from the editor's labels.
For `TRICOLON` the two disagree. The LR is 2.02 in the spec register,
but in the blog edit history most `TRICOLON` findings are accepted. In
TF-004 `list-like` and `enumeration` rose in arXiv abstracts and
`figure` was flat.
