## My Assessment

phase 1 accomplishments
hase 1 fixed a bug in how ticfinder reports where a finding is, and refreshed the
  saved harvest results so phase 2 starts from correct numbers.

  The bug. Each finding has two parts: the words it highlights (the span), and the
  sentence shown around them (the context). ticfinder takes the context from the
  sentence where the highlighted words begin. Two detectors could highlight words from
  a neighbouring sentence:

  - The emphatic reflexive detector (it flags "the tool itself") highlights the
    reflexive plus the three words before it. In "The run passed. The tool itself is
    fast.", those three words reach back to the full stop of "The run passed." The
    finding then showed the wrong sentence as its context, one that doesn't contain
    "itself" at all.
  - The "less about X than Y" detector highlights six words past "than", which could
    run into the next sentence.

  Each fingerprint is computed from the highlighted words and the context sentence, so
  a wrong context gives a wrong fingerprint.

  What I did:

     LLM corpora and in this repo's docs and the blogs. The reflexive bug turned up 23,

Phase 2 added the two new detectors from the design note. They report every finding
  at the lowest confidence until they have been measured, and adding them changed no
  existing finding.

  What the detectors look for. Both target one habit you keep rewriting: the verb comes
  at the end of a phrase, after the phrase's own subject. The reader has to hold a
  noun in mind until the verb arrives.

  - OBJECT_RELATIVE flags phrases like "every variable the loops assign". "Variable" is
    what gets assigned, but "assign" only arrives after "the loops". It has two
    versions:
    - the bare form, with no "that": "every variable the loops assign";
    - the form with "that" or "which": "every variable that the loops assign".

    The paper by Clark and colleagues found that LLMs overuse the bare form, so
    counting the two separately lets us compare them later.
  - TRAILING_LIGHT_VERB flags phrases like "the array write the last primitive made".
    The real action ("write") has been turned into a noun, and an empty verb ("made")
    closes the phrase.

  The load score. Each bare-form finding gets a load from 0 to 4: one point each for
  sitting after a preposition, a stacked or verb-like head noun, a long subject, and
  the verb ending the clause. The idea is that higher-load findings are the
  hard-to-read ones. The score is in each finding's label so phase 3 can test whether
  you rewrote high-load findings more often.

  Why everything is at low. The patch put some findings at med by judgement. The
  project's rule is that levels come from measurement, so both detectors start at low
  for now.

  Rewrite suggestions. With lemminflect installed, many OBJECT_RELATIVE findings now

  a problem in the patch: another finding covering exactly the same words could have
  shown the same suggestion. Now only OBJECT_RELATIVE findings get one.

  - The run takes the same time as before.

  What it doesn't tell us yet: whether these findings are right, and whether they match

## Background
TF-012's sanity reading found `EMPHATIC_REFLEXIVE` findings whose span
starts at the previous sentence's punctuation, with a context sentence
that lacks the match. A fingerprint hashes the construction, pattern,
matched text and sentence, so fixing a span changes the fingerprint of
each affected finding, and any waiver on one of them goes stale. The
hash itself does not change.

The committed `eval/labels.jsonl` and `eval/harvest-summary.json` hold
the totals from before TF-010 (141 accepted, 54 rewritten, 302
deleted). Harvest on a migrated waiver copy gives 132, 52 and 292 at
the TF-013 version (roadmap section 20). Earlier tasks forbade changing
these files; this task regenerates them once, at the phase 1 version.
`$PACINO/waivers` has not been migrated (19 entries are old-style ids),
so harvest runs on a migrated copy outside the repo, as in TF-010 to
TF-013.

Step 15 adds two constructions for a verb that ends its phrase after
the clause's own subject, so the reader holds a noun until the verb
arrives: "every variable the loops assign", "the array write the last
primitive made". Clark, Martin and Mahowald (2026, arXiv:2610.29162)
find that frontier models overuse reduced object relatives (no "that")
and underuse ones with "that". The patch matches POS sequences because
spaCy misparses exactly these sentences. It was built against c0d95d4,
its new test rows were run in isolation (48 checks) and the full suite
was never run on it. Its levels (`med` for `TRAILING_LIGHT_VERB` and
for `OBJECT_RELATIVE` at load 2 or more) were set by judgement; step
15 starts both at `low` until measured LRs set them.

Harvest can recover rewritten findings from old drafts, but only
waivers mark a finding accepted, so blogs edited before this step give
the new constructions rewritten and open labels and no accepted ones.
