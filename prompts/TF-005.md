# Task Overview

Roadmap next step 9: a pilot that extends the TF-004 arXiv comparison
from abstracts to paper bodies. About 200 `cs.AR` papers per window,
drawn from the TF-004 set, are measured from their LaTeX source, section
by section, next to the same papers' abstracts. The pilot asks whether
the abstract ratios hold for whole papers and where in a paper the
changes concentrate. It also gives `EM_DASH` a measurement and gives
intervals to constructions that were too rare in abstracts.

Two phases with a stop between them: phase 1 fetches the source and
builds and checks the LaTeX masker; phase 2 runs the analysis.

● TF-005 synopsis

  TF-004 measured ticfinder constructions in arXiv cs.AR abstracts before and after
  LLMs. TF-005 asked whether those changes also hold in the full papers.

  What was built

  - eval/arxiv_bodies.py, a new script that:
    - downloads the LaTeX source of each paper as first submitted;
    - finds the main file and pulls in its included files;
    - blanks LaTeX markup in place, so text positions still line up;
    - splits each paper into introduction, body, related work and conclusion;
    - runs ticfinder on each paper and on its abstract, and reports rates and ratios
      per section.
  - The masker is checked by a 64-row self-test on made-up LaTeX.
  - All paper text and findings stay in private/. The committed files hold only totals
    and rates.

  The sample

  - 200 papers before LLMs and 200 after, drawn at random from the TF-004 papers.
  - That is about 2.2 million words of paper text, against 76k words of abstracts.
  - The pilot's own pass conditions were met: at least 150 usable papers per window,
    and leftover LaTeX in no more than 2 of 40 checked sentences. Leftover LaTeX turned
    up in 1 of 40 sentences before and 0 of 40 after.

  Findings

  - The hypothesis holds. For 5 of 7 constructions, the change in full papers matches
    the change in abstracts.
    - TRICOLON misses only because of the 200-abstract sample. Its full-paper ratio
      agrees with the full TF-004 value.
    - ABSTRACT_ADVERB really did rise more in full papers.
    - The test is weak, because 200 abstracts give wide intervals.
  - The alternative is not supported. LLM influence is not confined to abstracts and
    introductions. Ratios in the main body sections are as large or larger.
  - Largest rises in full papers (ratio of post rate to pre rate):

## Hypothesis
The construction ratios measured on abstracts in TF-004 also hold for
the bodies of the same papers. For each construction and pattern with at
least 20 findings in both windows of both text types, the body ratio
lies inside the abstract ratio's 95% interval.

The alternative, from published work on LLM use in papers: LLM editing
concentrates in abstracts and introductions. Then introduction ratios
match the abstract ratios and body ratios are smaller. Either result is
useful, and the report says which one the data shows.

The hypothesis fails if most constructions with enough findings have a
body ratio outside the abstract interval. The pilot fails as a pilot,
without answering the question, if masking leaves LaTeX residue in more
than 2 of 40 spot-checked sentences per window, or if fewer than 150
papers per window have usable source. Report either outcome as found.

## Background
TF-004 measured 955 and 2,627 primary `cs.AR` abstracts. Abstracts are
about 190 words, so the pre window held 179k words, which set most
interval widths. Several constructions landed in "too few", and
`EM_DASH` found nothing because the API serves ASCII abstracts. A paper
body holds 5,000 to 10,000 words of prose, and its LaTeX source has the
dashes the author typed. The introduction, design and evaluation
sections of an architecture paper are also closer to the design
register ticfinder targets than an abstract is.

The post window still mixes human-written and LLM-assisted papers, and
topic shifted toward ML and LLM hardware (section 11). The pilot does
not remove either effect; it reports the topic caveat as TF-004 did.

  included as images), under 300 words after masking, other format.
- Text before the first `\section` counts as introduction.
- Ties between main-file candidates go to the longest expanded text.
- `\IEEEPARstart{T}{he}` and `\textbf{S}ystem` keep their letters
  next to the rest of the word. The letters move a few characters
  inside the blanked command; offsets outside it hold.

  dropped.
