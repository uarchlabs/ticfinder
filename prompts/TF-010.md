# Task Overview

TF-009's sanity reading found three detectors that fire mostly on
ordinary normative spec language: `CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST` (25 of 100
findings real), `NEG_ESCALATION` (2 of 68) and the `af_less_than`
pattern of `ALTERNATIVE_FRAMING` (mostly comparisons of quantities).
Step 8 computes LRs for the spec register, so these detectors need
fixing first, or their LRs measure spec phrasing. This task adds test
rows for each failure kind, narrows the detectors, and measures the
change on the blogs, the public spec baseline and the arXiv bodies. It
then reruns the spec baseline and the arXiv body analysis at the new
version, so step 8 reads both at one ticfinder version.

Three phases with a stop after each:

- Phase 1: baselines and test rows (rows only, no detector change).
- Phase 2: the detector changes, measured one pattern at a time.
- Phase 3: reruns at the new version, reports and records.
