# Task Overview

TF-009's sanity reading found three detectors that fire mostly on
ordinary normative spec language: `CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST` (25 of 100
findings real), `NEG_ESCALATION` (2 of 68) and the `af_less_than`
pattern of `ALTERNATIVE_FRAMING` (mostly comparisons of quantities).
Step 8 computes LRs for the spec register, so these detectors need
fixing first, or their LRs measure spec phrasing. This task adds test
rows for each failure kind, narrows the detectors, and measures the
change on the blogs, the public spec baseline and the arXiv bodies. It
then reruns the spec baseline and the arXiv body analysis at the new
version, so step 8 reads both at one ticfinder version.

Three phases with a stop after each:

- Phase 1: baselines and test rows (rows only, no detector change).
- Phase 2: the detector changes, measured one pattern at a time.
- Phase 3: reruns at the new version, reports and records.

In `tools/ticfinder.py` the detectors are `cc_coordinated`, `cc_cleft`,
`af_less_than` and `neg_escalation`. `cc_parallel_sibling` and

- `cc_coordinated` anchors on any negation and looks for a `but` or
  `rather` coordination within four hops. It does not check that the
  negation sits in the first conjunct, so `allowed but not required`
  fires. It also fires on additive `not only X but also Y`. The
  existing positive row `not merely a technical choice but an
  architectural commitment` is corrective and must keep firing.
- `cc_cleft` pairs a negated copula with a later copula within 22
  tokens, across a sentence break, when the second copula's subject is
  a pronoun or determiner. It fires when the second clause is a new
  statement and not a correction: `this may not be possible.` followed
  by an unrelated `It is ...`, a causal or concessive link (`so`,
  `since`, `though`), or a second copula inside a relative clause.
- `af_less_than` fires on `less` or `more` followed by `than` within 14
  tokens, with no other check. Its docstring intends `less about X than
  about Y`.
- `neg_escalation` takes a negated head with a subjectless negated
  modal conjunct. It does not check that the conjunct is gapped, so
  `MUST NOT add X and MUST return Y` and `shall not read the CSR and
  shall not cause any side effects` fire. Real cases repeat or elide
  the first verb: `did not catch this, and could not have`.

The TF-009 per-document findings are in

## Background
TF-009 (roadmap section 16) built the public spec baseline for step 8
and read 20 findings per source for four rows. Real instances out of
those read, per source (PEPs, IETF, Rust, RISC-V, OpenTitan):
`CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST` 8, 4, 2, 6 and 5 of 20; `NEG_ESCALATION` 1 of 9,
0 of 20, 1 of 11, 0 of 7 and 0 of 1; `ALTERNATIVE_FRAMING` 17, 12, 16,
15 and 4 of 20, where nearly every miss was `af_less_than` on a count,
a size or a privilege level. TF-009 counted a comparison of two
options (`more direct than checking a version`) as real. This task
uses the narrower reading in the `af_less_than` docstring; see the
binding decisions.

The pool over arXiv ratios in TF-009 were `NEG_ESCALATION` 5.63 (2.82
to 10.93) and `CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST` 2.78 (2.47 to 3.11), the first and
third largest. Some of that gap is detector noise that normative text
sets off more often than papers do.

## Binding Previous Decisions
- CLAUDE.md "How to work": measure every detector change with
  `make regress` and harvest before and after, test row first, and
  stop at the end of each phase with files changed, `make regress`,
  harvest numbers and a suggested commit message. The user commits.
- Test rows are written from synthetic sentences in the failure kinds.
  No sentence from the public corpus, the arXiv bodies or the author's
  specs goes into `tests.py` or any committed file.
- A construction id stays; patterns change. Anything a user would mute
  separately gets its own pattern. The additive `not only X but also Y`
  form becomes its own pattern under `CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST`, so it is
  measured and muted on its own and not dropped.
- A new or renamed pattern changes fingerprints. Add it to
  `RENAMED_PATTERNS` so `--migrate-waivers` can rekey old waivers, and
  report how many waivers it would move, on a copy.
- `af_less_than` targets the framing of one description against
  another: `less about X than about Y`, `less a bug than a feature`,
  `more a convention than a rule`. Plain comparatives of degree or
  quantity (`more than one`, `less than 8`, `less privileged than M`,
  `more power than desired`, `more expressive than Y`) are out.
- Confidence levels do not change in this task. Step 8 sets them from
  LRs.
- The bootstrap and seeds are TF-009's (spec baseline seed 9009) and
  TF-008's (arXiv seed 8008), so the reruns differ from the old reports
  only through the code.
- Third-party text and anything derived from it that holds sentences
  stays under `private/` and on the console.
