# Task Overview

Roadmap step 7: collect the public design and spec baseline. Step 8
(per-pattern LRs for the design and spec registers) needs a human
baseline in the same register as the `pacino` LLM specs. The author's
pre-LLM specs hold about 89k words after masking, too few on their own.
This task builds a public pre-LLM corpus from five sources, masks it,
checks the masking per file, and measures construction rates with
intervals. It also compares those rates with the author's specs and with
the TF-008 arXiv pre-window bodies. It sets no confidence level and
computes no LR; that is step 8.

Three phases with a stop after each:

- Phase 1: preconditions, the TF-004 check fix TF-008 deferred, and the
  source survey and fetch.
- Phase 2: per-source exclusions and masking, and the masking checks.
- Phase 3: analyse, write the report and its JSON, and test the
  hypotheses.
