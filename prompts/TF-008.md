# Task Overview

Roadmap step 14: the full arXiv body run. The TF-005 pilot measured
200 papers per window. This task measures every pre-window paper with
usable LaTeX source (about 775 of 955) and the same number of
post-window papers, with the ticfinder that TF-006 and TF-007 fixed
and split. One analysis then measures the fixed and split patterns,
narrows the pilot's intervals, and checks the TF-007 interim levels on
four times the pre-window words.

Three phases with a stop after each:

- Phase 1: preliminaries. JSON summaries from harvest and both arXiv
  scripts (the step 8 prerequisite), a snapshot of the pilot tables,
  and two cleanups left by TF-007.
- Phase 2: extend the fetch to the full sample and check the masking.
- Phase 3: analyse, write the full report, and test the hypotheses.

## Hypothesis
H1. The pilot was a fair sample. For most constructions and patterns
with at least 20 findings in both runs, the full-run body ratio (all
sections) lies inside the pilot's 95% interval.

H2. The larger pre sample narrows the intervals. Pre words grow about
four times, so the body interval width (on the log scale) shrinks by
roughly half for rows dominated by the pre side.

H3. The TF-007 levels hold. `rather than` and the six raised phrase
forms (`comprehensive`, `essential`, `landscape`, `leverages`,
`leveraging`, `optimizing`) still rise in bodies, and `instead of` and
lemma `use` stay flat.

H4. Bodies alone are enough for the interim rule. With the full run,
the seven phrases that rose in pilot bodies but were flat in abstracts
(`crucial`, `robust`, `seamless`, `innovative`, `harness`, `dynamics`,
`optimized`) still rise in bodies. If so, step 8 can use body ratios
alone as its prior. H4 fails if most of the seven become flat.

Report each outcome as found, including null and failed hypotheses.

## Background
TF-005 (roadmap section 12) recommended a full run after steps 10 and
11, so that one analysis measures the fixed and split patterns. Both
steps are done (TF-006, TF-007). The pilot intervals are wide because
the pre window has fewer papers: 200 pre papers hold 1.30 million body
words. The rare rows (`HEDGE_STACK`, `GAPPED_ANTITHESIS`,
`cc_parallel_sibling`, `DISGUISE_METAPHOR`) and most subtypes had too
few findings to measure.

TF-007 set interim confidence levels by a rule that needs a rise in
both TF-004 abstracts and TF-005 bodies. Seven of the eight phrases on
which the corpora disagreed rose in bodies only, which suggests bodies
are the more sensitive corpus. Step 8 (calibration) will use body
ratios as its prior, and roadmap section 7 lists JSON summaries from
harvest and the arXiv scripts as its prerequisite.

`VAGUE_QUANTIFIER` `increasingly` rose 1.99 in TF-004 abstracts. Its
body ratio has not been computed.
