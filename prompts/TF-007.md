  confidence per finding.
- `fingerprint()` hashes construction, pattern, matched text and
  sentence. Confidence is not hashed.
- `legacy_fingerprint()` and `--migrate-waivers` rekey waivers whose id
  was computed the old way. That is the model for a pattern-rename
  migration.
- Phrase-list constructions are compiled by `phrase_patterns()` and
  matched under one Matcher key per construction in `analyse()`. Their
  findings have an empty pattern name. `load_phrase_file()` reads one

## Hypothesis

## Background
TF-004 (roadmap section 11) found that the change over time differs
more within these constructions than between them. `rather than` rose
3.84 (2.29 to 8.06) in abstracts while `instead of` was flat at 1.07.
The blog labels agree: by subtype, `rather than` has prec* 0.54 and
`instead of` 0.12. TF-005 bodies give 4.60 (3.22 to 6.70) and 0.98.
CLAUDE.md says to group ids by measured precision, and that anything a
user would mute separately needs its own id or pattern. `--off` mutes
by construction or pattern name, not subtype.

`PARTICIPIAL_TAIL` is accurate in both corpora (17/20 and 20/20 real
in abstracts, 18/20 and 20/20 in bodies). Its verbs differ in how much
they rose, not in precision. By the CLAUDE.md rule that is a confidence
question, not a new pattern. In abstracts, `enable` rose 6.56,
`achieve` 4.45 and `demonstrate` 8.10, and `use`, `result` and `allow`
were flat. In bodies, `allow` rose 7.77, `make` 5.82 and `result` 3.52,
and only `use` was flat (1.33).

`LLM_DICTION` and `CONSULTANT_REGISTER` are phrase lists in
`ticfinder-phrases.json` at `low` confidence. `CONSULTANT_REGISTER`
uses `match_lemma`, so the entry `leverage` matches `leverages` (3.54,
rose), `leveraging` (1.88, rose) and `leverage` (0.83, flat). One
confidence per entry cannot tell these forms apart.

The convention in CLAUDE.md is that confidence comes from measured
likelihood ratios once calibration lands (roadmap 5.3, step 8). That
has not happened yet. This task sets interim confidences from an
explicit rule over the arXiv ratios, and step 8 replaces them.
