# Task Overview

Roadmap step 8, first of two tasks. It builds the LLM side of the
design and spec register from the `pacino` specs and design notes, and
measures a likelihood ratio (LR) per construction and pattern against
the TF-009 public baseline, with the author's specs as a check and the
arXiv change as a prior. It writes a report and JSON and proposes
confidence bands from the data. It changes no confidence level and no
detector; a later task applies the bands the user picks.

Three phases with a stop after each:

- Phase 1: the corpus. File list, exclusions and masking checks, with
  the user's approval of the list before any measurement.
- Phase 2: the measurement. Per-document tables, LRs with intervals, the
  author's-spec check and the shrinkage toward the arXiv prior.
- Phase 3: the report, the JSON, proposed bands and records.

## Background
Confidence levels are set by judgement and by the TF-007 interim rule
over the arXiv ratios (roadmap section 14). Section 5.3 replaces them
with a measured LR per pattern and register:

```
LR(pattern) = rate in LLM text of register R / rate in human text of register R
```

TF-009 built the human side for the design and spec register: 602
documents from five sources, 1.82M words after exclusions and masking,
with an equal-weight pool and a document-level bootstrap (seed 9009).
TF-010 reran it at 494e4dd. TF-008 measured the arXiv `cs.AR` body
change over 1,556 papers (seed 8008), rerun at 494e4dd by TF-010. The
author's 36 pre-LLM spec files (about 82k words after masking) are a
check on the pool in the exact domain; TF-009 found they agree with the
pool on 5 of 9 rows.

Known precision limits carry into the LRs. TF-010 and TF-011 found 49%
of the pool's `cc_coordinated` and `cc_cleft` findings real; concessive
pairs inflate the human rate, so the `CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST` LR is biased
low. `NEG_ESCALATION` and `af_less_than` have under 20 findings in the
pool.

### Phase 2: the measurement

6. **Tables.** Run ticfinder over the kept text at nice 19 on one
   process, writing per-document counts and findings to
   `private/llm-specs/`. Report run time and words per file.
7. **LRs.** For each construction and pattern row: the LLM rate, the
   pool rate, LR = LLM over pool with a 95% interval from a joint
   bootstrap (LLM documents with seed 12012, pool documents with seed
   9009, 2,000 draws), and the LR against the author's specs. Rows under
   20 findings on either side get no LR.
8. **Prior.** Shrink each log LR toward the log arXiv post over pre
   ratio with a normal-normal empirical Bayes step: the prior variance is
   estimated across rows by the method of moments, and each row's weight
   comes from its bootstrap variance. Report raw and shrunk LRs side by
   side, and say how far the shrinkage moves rows under 50 findings.
9. **Checks.** H2, H3 and H4 from the tables. Flag rows with known
   precision limits (`CORRECTIVE_CONTRAST` and its patterns) rather than
   dropping them.
10. **Sanity reading.** 20 random LLM-corpus findings for each of the 5
    rows with the highest LR and the 5 with the lowest, counting real
    instances (console only, counts in the records).
11. **Stop.** Summarise as CLAUDE.md asks, with the LR table.
