In `tools/ticfinder.py` the code in scope is `cc_coordinated` and its

are out of scope.

- A concessive pair negates the first conjunct and then asserts
  something that does not take the negated element's place:
  `is not fully general, but covers most calls`, `is not compilable
  but serves to demonstrate the interface`, `does not propose a
  mechanism, but tries to stay compatible with one`. It concedes a
  limit and moves on. It corrects nothing.
- A corrective pair puts the second conjunct in the negated element's
  slot: `does not pass the exact size, but rather the number of
  pages`, `does not use reference counting, but a tracing collector`,
  `is not built into the protocol but is instead left to the client`,
  `uses the format not for distribution but as an exchange format`.
- Some real corrective pairs repeat or change the verb without a
  marker (`do not use X, but use Y`; `should not modify the values,
  but pass them along`; `is not managed by the client but is validated
  by the server`). A cue that drops every verb-headed second conjunct
  will lose these. That loss is what the measurement must show.
